# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in SelectiveLLM respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and evidence-led.
- Critique claims and methods without attacking people.
- Welcome negative results and good-faith replication attempts.
- Disclose conflicts, benchmark limitations, and licensing constraints.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing credentials, private data, or restricted model artifacts.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and deliberate research misrepresentation are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository security contact. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct threatens a safe, rigorous community. Enforcement decisions should protect reporters' privacy and be proportionate to the behavior.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
